Theoretical Review and Justification of Multiculturalism

多元文化主义的理论检视与正当化

초록

Abstract

The object of this study is to present a legitimate logic of multiculturalism through examining the existing theoretical controversy on both the pro and contra sides. Multiculturalism is based on the inevitable cultural plurality in human society, and as a cognitive framework it plays a positive role in a democratic and diverse society under globalization: it contributes to enhancing equality, promotes the integration of residents with diverse ethnic, language and religious backgrounds, and represents a deep concern for the inherent rights of human beings.
