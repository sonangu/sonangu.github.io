Multicultural Education in the U.S.: Theory and Practice

美国多元文化教育的理论与实践

초록

Abstract

Multicultural education in the United States is a good reference for designing Korean multicultural education programs and curricula, yet research on the characteristics and development of multicultural education in the U.S. is rare in Korea. This article analyzes changes in the U.S. population composition, the progress of multiculturalism, and the reality of multicultural education, and discusses the implications for Korean multicultural education.
